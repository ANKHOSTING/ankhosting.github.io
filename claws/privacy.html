Claws of Jarvis Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Claws of Jarvis ("the app", "we") is a multi-model AI chat workspace published by ankhosting, available on the web at ankhosting.online and as an Android app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Contact: ankhosting2025@gmail.com

1. No account

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address or contacts.

2. Data stored on your device

Your chat history, task items, selected model and plan, daily usage counts and credit balance are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage so your workspace can be restored. If the saved state has not been updated for 30 days, it is discarded the next time the app loads. The Android app also keeps up to 20 recent Google Play purchase tokens on your device so the same purchase is not granted twice. Clearing site or app data, or uninstalling the app, removes this data.

3. AI chat

When you send a message, your conversation (up to 60 messages) and the model you selected are sent over HTTPS to our server (hosted on Vercel) and forwarded to OpenRouter, which routes the request to the selected model's provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek or Meta) to generate a reply. Our server does not store your messages or the replies. To limit abuse, it keeps your IP address with a request count in temporary server memory for a one-minute rate-limit window. OpenRouter and the model providers handle requests under their own policies. Do not enter passwords, secrets or sensitive personal information into chats.

4. Purchases (Android)

Purchases are handled by Google Play. When you buy a plan or credit pack, the app sends the product ID and Google Play purchase token to our server, which checks them with Google Play to confirm the purchase. Our server does not store the token, and we never receive your payment card details.

5. Analytics

The web app uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. Vercel states that it does not use cookies and records anonymous data points (such as page URL, referrer, approximate geolocation, device type, operating system and browser), identifying a visit by a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours.

6. Advertising

The app does not show ads and does not include advertising SDKs.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app: Vercel (hosting and analytics), OpenRouter and the selected model provider (AI chat), and Google Play (purchases). We do not sell personal data.

8. Retention and deletion

We do not keep chat content on our servers, so there is no server-side history to delete. Data on your device is kept until it expires (30 days for saved workspace state), you clear site or app data, or you uninstall the app. For any privacy request, email ankhosting2025@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Third-party policies

  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy · Vercel: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Vercel Web Analytics: https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy · Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
