Delete your Urban Sprout account and data

Urban Sprout (com.ankhosting.urbansprout) is published by ankhosting. You can delete your account and its data yourself inside the app, or ask us to do it by email.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open Urban Sprout and sign in.
  2. Tap the Account tab (person icon, bottom right).
  3. Tap the red Delete account button.
  4. In the "Delete account?" dialog, tap Delete forever. This cannot be undone.

The app first removes your uploaded photos, then deletes your account, and signs you out. If you see an error, check your connection and try again.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app (for example you uninstalled it or forgot your password), email ankhosting2025@gmail.com with the subject Urban Sprout account deletion, from the email address you registered with. We will reply to confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days of confirmation.

What is kept, and for how long

  • AI questions: the text of your questions and answers is not stored by us (only a per-day count, which is deleted with your account).
  • Purchase records: if you subscribed to Sprout Pro, Google Play and RevenueCat keep billing and purchase records under their own policies and legal obligations. We cannot delete those. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it first in Google Play (Manage subscription, or Urban Sprout → Account → Manage subscription).
  • Ad data: Google AdMob collects advertising data directly on your device (see our privacy policy). It is not stored in your Urban Sprout account; manage it in your Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • On your device: watering reminders you scheduled are stored on your phone and are not removed by account deletion. Uninstall the app (or switch each plant's reminders off first) to remove them.
