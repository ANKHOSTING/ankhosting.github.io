TextCraft: Word Counter Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

TextCraft: Word Counter ("the app", "we") is published by ankhosting. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Contact: ankhosting2025@gmail.com

1. No account

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address or contacts.

2. Your text stays on your device

Text you type or paste, and drafts you save, are processed and stored on your device (local app storage). Word counts, character counts, keyword density, readability and time estimates are calculated on your device without an internet connection. Deleting a draft, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

3. AI writing check (optional, only when you tap it)

When you tap AI writing check, the text in the editor (up to the first 6,000 characters) is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server (hosted on Supabase) and forwarded to an AI model provider (currently Fireworks AI, via an OpenAI-compatible API) to generate corrections. Fireworks AI states that, by default, it does not log or store prompt or generation data for open models (requests are held only in memory while being processed, plus up to several minutes in a prompt cache) and logs only request metadata such as token counts; see the Fireworks data handling docs and privacy policy. We do not store your text or the AI's response. To apply the free daily limit, our server stores a random identifier that the app generates on first use (not linked to your identity) together with a per-day count of checks, and a keyed (HMAC) hash of your IP address (never the IP address itself) with a per-day request count, to prevent abuse. If you have a Pro subscription, the app also sends an anonymous RevenueCat app user ID so the server can confirm Pro status.

4. Purchases

TextCraft Pro is a subscription handled by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase records and an anonymous app user ID to confirm your subscription. We never see your payment card details.

5. Advertising

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), a Google consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads are requested, and ads are not requested until consent is handled. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still collect your IP address, your Android advertising ID, interaction information and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. TextCraft Pro removes ads.

6. What we do not collect

The app does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library access for any feature.

7. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app: Supabase (server hosting), Fireworks AI or the configured AI provider (AI check), RevenueCat and Google Play (subscriptions), and Google AdMob (ads). We do not sell personal data.

8. Security

Data sent to our server and providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Retention and deletion

Drafts are kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Because there are no accounts, there is no account to delete. Usage counters are tied only to a random identifier, are keyed by day, contain no text, and are deleted automatically after a short period (about 2 days in current releases, and never more than 30 days). To ask us to delete the usage counter associated with your device or for any other privacy request, email ankhosting2025@gmail.com.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Third-party policies

  • Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy · Fireworks AI: https://fireworks.ai/privacy-policy
  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy · Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
