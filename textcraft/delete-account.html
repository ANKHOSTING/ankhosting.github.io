Delete your TextCraft data

TextCraft: Word Counter (com.textcounter.app) is published by ankhosting. The app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Here is how to remove the data that exists.

Your text and saved drafts (on your device only)

  1. Open TextCraft and tap the Drafts tab.
  2. Tap Delete next to a draft (or long-press the draft) and confirm Delete.
  3. To clear the editor, go to the Write tab and tap Clear.

To remove everything at once, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → TextCraft → Storage → Clear data. Drafts never leave your device, so we hold no copy of them.

AI writing check data

When you tap AI writing check, your text (up to 6,000 characters) is sent to our server and an AI provider to generate corrections. We do not store your text or the result.

To apply the free daily limit our server keeps a random device identifier that the app generates (it is not linked to your name or email and is not shown in the app) with a per-day count of checks. These records are deleted after 30 days. If you also want them removed sooner, email ankhosting2025@gmail.com with the subject TextCraft data request. Because the identifier is random and not visible to you, we may not be able to locate your specific record; uninstalling the app and reinstalling creates a new identifier.
